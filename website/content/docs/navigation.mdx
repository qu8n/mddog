## Contents

Press `t` to show the table of contents sidebar.

Click an entry to jump to it. You don't need the panel open to jump between sections with key bindings:

- `1` to `9` jump to the Nth top-level section. Press the same digit again to step through its subsections, or press `0` first to step backward
- `J` and `K` (or `]]` and `[[`) go to the next and previous section

## Links

Click a link to follow it:

- A web link opens in your browser, and a `mailto:` link in your mail app
- A `#heading` link scrolls to that heading
- A relative link to a `.md` or `.markdown` file opens it in mddog, at the heading if the link names one
- A link to a folder opens its `README.md` or `README.markdown`, as GitHub does

Press `Backspace` or `Ctrl-o` to go back. This also undoes jumps from the contents, search, and section keys, up to 50 steps.

### Safety guardrails

- A link opens only Markdown files, and only inside the current repo
- With no repo, the limit is your home folder. A link to any other local file shows a notice and does nothing
- Links with other schemes, such as `javascript:`, also do nothing
