---
title: Table of contents
description: Jump between sections with the table of contents and section keys.
---

Press `t` to show the table of contents sidebar.

Click an entry to jump to it. You don't need the panel open to jump between sections with key bindings:

- `1` to `9` jump to the Nth top-level section. Press the same digit again to step through its subsections, or press `0` first to step backward
- `J` and `K` (or `]]` and `[[`) go to the next and previous section
