title: Terminals

mddog sends the page to the terminal as images and thus requires the terminal to support the [kitty graphics protocol](https://sw.kovidgoyal.net/kitty/graphics-protocol/).

## Supported

| Terminal | Notes |
|---|---|
| [Ghostty](https://ghostty.org) | Everything works |
| [kitty](https://sw.kovidgoyal.net/kitty/) | Everything works |
| [WezTerm](https://wezterm.org) | [`Cmd-c`](#copy-text-selection-with-cmd-c) needs a setting to work, and the [mouse pointer](#mouse-pointer) doesn't change shape over links |
| [iTerm2](https://iterm2.com) | [`Cmd-c`](#copy-text-selection-with-cmd-c) can't reach mddog. Set `copy_on_select` in your config as a workaround |
| Rex | Everything works |

## Copy text selection with Cmd-c

Some terminals keep `Cmd-c` for themselves, so it never reaches mddog. To work around this:

- In WezTerm, set `enable_kitty_keyboard = true` and remove WezTerm's `Cmd-c` (`SUPER+c`) binding
- In iTerm2, set [`copy_on_select = true`](/docs/config#copy_on_select) in mddog's config so that text selection is automatically copied to the clipboard

## Mouse pointer

The pointer turns into a hand over links and buttons in Ghostty, kitty, and iTerm2. This doesn't work in WezTerm and herdr.

## Multiplexers

### herdr

mddog works inside herdr, but images reach every terminal except Ghostty slowly ([herdr#5512](https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr/issues/5512)). Starting herdr with `TERM_PROGRAM=ghostty herdr` fixes the lag, but then herdr also treats the terminal as Ghostty for notifications and focus.

Under herdr with Ghostty, a copy larger than about 220 KB doesn't reach the clipboard over OSC 52. This only matters over SSH. On the same Mac, mddog also copies with `pbcopy`.

When herdr runs inside Warp, mddog can't detect Warp and runs anyway. There, scrolling doesn't move the page, and `G` draws it squashed.

### Rex

Rex has its own panes and tabs, and mddog works in them.

## Not supported

In these terminals, mddog stops with an error:

- Terminal.app: can't show images
- screen: can't show images
- tmux: can't show kitty graphics yet ([tmux#5445](https://github.com/tmux/tmux/pull/5445))
- Warp: can't crop images, and mddog crops images to scroll ([Warp#16424](https://github.com/warpdotdev/Warp/issues/16424))
- zellij: garbles scrolled images ([zellij#5573](https://github.com/zellij-org/zellij/issues/5573))
