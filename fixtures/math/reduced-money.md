# Plain dollars next to math

A coffee costs $5 and a sandwich costs $10, so lunch is $15 in total.

The budget is $5 and $10 for two people, and the sum $5 + $10 is not math.

Prices like $1,200.50 or 3$ are also just text. Mixed: the fee is $20, but $x + y = 20$ is math.

A tricky one: it costs $5, and the discount is $\alpha = 0.1$ off.

Trailing: we paid $ 5 and then $ 6 later.

Escaped: it costs \$5 and \$10, and `$5 and $10` in code stays literal.
